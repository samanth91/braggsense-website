Built by people who have lived the measurement problem.

We design fiber Bragg grating sensing systems and the software that makes them usable on a mill, a bridge, or a test article. The WT-04 optical-fibre monitor is the proof we ship in the browser.

What we believe

A sensor that cannot be trusted in the field is a laboratory toy. A dashboard that cannot be understood in a glance is a missed alarm. We own both ends of that chain: the grating, the interrogator, and BraggSoft.

We are independent. We recommend the fibre count and channel count the structure needs — not a catalogue maximum.
